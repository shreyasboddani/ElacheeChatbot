Upcoming Events
Young Explorers Club
January 06 - May 28, 2026
Adventure awaits every week at the Young Explorers Club!
This hands-on program invites children to learn, discover, and explore through a blend of indoor activities, outdoor adventures or guided hikes, and an animal experience with one of Elachee’s resident ambassadors. Activities vary from session to session, so each visit brings something new and exciting.
Don’t miss the chance to experience Elachee’s brand-new Discovery Dome!
Select Young Explorers Club sessions will include special time inside the Dome on January 6, January 8, and January 22.
Two age groups participate during each session to ensure an engaging, age-appropriate experience:
Ages 3–6
Ages 7–12
NOTE: Families should be aware that siblings may be placed in different groups based on age. Please purchase tickets that match each child’s age.
Young Explorers Club is designed to spark curiosity and build confidence in nature. From animal encounters to themed activities and nature play, children will enjoy a meaningful outdoor learning experience in a fun, supportive environment.
Parents and guardians must remain on campus for the duration of the program. Pre-registration is encouraged; walk-ins are welcome based on availability.
This program will take place rain or shine. Please dress appropriately for the forecasted weather.
Special thanks to our 2025 Platinum Annual Sponsor Northside Hospital and our 2025 Silver Annual Sponsors Chattahoochee Marketing Group, Fieldale Farms Corporation, Mincey Marble Manufacturing, and Smith Gilliam Williams & Miles.
Date: Jan 06 - May 28, 2026
Time: 9 AM - 11 AM
Price: $9.60/members, $12/non-members
Category: General Admission
