## Curated public facts

## Where to go

Elachee Nature Science Center's main campus and Visitor Center are at 2125 Elachee Drive, Gainesville, GA 30504. Elachee advises visitors to enter the full street address in GPS because address autofill may lead to the wrong location.

Chicopee Lake and the Aquatic Studies Center are at 2100 Calvary Church Road, Gainesville, GA 30507. This is a separate location and trail access point; use the destination that matches the program or trailhead you plan to visit.

## What to bring

Elachee recommends a packed lunch, bug spray, comfortable shoes, sun protection, weather layers, water, and snacks. Shaded picnic areas, the Visitor Center patio, and the Nature Nook reading platform are places to take a break. Pack out food and other items. There are no water fountains on the trails.

Visitors can ask about a Discovery Backpack at the Visitor Center; it contains tools and activity prompts for a nature scavenger hunt. Visitor Center restrooms are available only during Visitor Center public hours; portable toilets are located at trailheads.

The Visitor Center/exhibits and hiking trails keep separate hours. See https://elachee.org/hours. Parking is separate from Visitor Center admission and free trail access; see https://elachee.org/parking-admissions.
