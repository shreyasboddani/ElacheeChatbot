## Curated public facts

Elachee offers several distinct nature-based programs. Their schedules, age ranges, registration dates, and availability differ; use the individual program page or current event listing for a particular session.

- Nature Academy: a tuition-based, Cognia-accredited, full-time nature-based school serving children ages 3 through first grade. Elachee describes weekday learning during the academic year.
- Camp Elachee: weeklong summer day-camp sessions, generally Monday through Friday in June and July, for kindergarten through middle-school children. The age cutoff, session dates, and open spaces are year-specific; confirm them on the current Camp Elachee registration page.
- Sprouts: monthly, caregiver-and-child nature-play sessions for toddlers ages 18 months to 4 years, with movement, music, stories, and sensory exploration.
- Homeschool Days: monthly, hands-on, nature-based science classes for homeschool families; topics change. Elachee's overview lists K-12, while its dedicated Homeschool page says ages 6-12. Because those official descriptions differ, do not promise a specific eligibility range; check the current session listing or confirm with Elachee.

Program fees and registration are separate from general Visitor Center admission and may vary by activity. Current overview: https://elachee.org/camps-programs
