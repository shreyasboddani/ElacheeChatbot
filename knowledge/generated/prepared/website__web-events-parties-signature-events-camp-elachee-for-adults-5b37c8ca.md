Camp Elachee for Adults
Yes, Grown-Ups Get to Play Too
Slip into the forest for a day of real connection, good food, and grown-up fun — with just the right amount of nostalgia.
You Need a Break That Actually Feels Like One
Life’s loud. You’re busy. And sometimes, “self-care” feels like just another task.
Camp Elachee for Adults is your permission slip to slow down, laugh a little louder, and rediscover the joy of being outside.
Why It Matters
Find Your Way
Back to the Woods
Because nature isn’t just for kids. It’s for anyone who needs a reset, a breath of fresh air, and a day that actually feels like summer.
What’s Happening at Elachee
Our recurring experiences invite you to return again and again, with the individual sessions that explore science, nature, creativity, and discovery.
