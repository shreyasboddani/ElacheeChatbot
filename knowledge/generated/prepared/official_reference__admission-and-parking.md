## Curated public facts

Visitor Center admission, hiking-trail access, and vehicle parking are separate costs.

## Visitor Center and exhibits

- General admission is $10 per person for ages 4 and older.
- The family admission maximum is $50.
- Admission is free for Elachee members.
- Some programs and special activities require separate registration or fees.

## Chicopee Woods hiking trails

Trail access is free. Vehicle parking is charged separately and is managed by the Chicopee Woods Area Park Commission.

- Daily parking: $5 per vehicle.
- 15-passenger vans and buses: $10.
- Annual parking pass: $50.
- Elachee members receive a free annual parking pass.
