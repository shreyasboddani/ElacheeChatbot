Homeschool Days at Elachee
Where Science Steps Off the Page
Real Learning. Real Nature.
Elachee Homeschool Days bring science to life through monthly, nature-based programs designed especially for homeschooling families. Perfect for ages 6-12, Elachee Homeschool Days combine outdoor adventure with curriculum-aligned topics. From wildlife and ecology to geology and plant biology, each session offers hands-on exploration guided by expert educators. Every session is a chance to ask questions, follow curiosity, and learn how the natural world really works.
Elachee Homeschool Days go beyond the basics, encouraging collaboration, observation, and independent thinking in a dynamic outdoor setting. It’s a great way to supplement your curriculum, foster social connection, and let your child experience science as something active, real, and wildly fun. You’ll leave with muddy boots, happy kids, and a notebook full of new ideas.
