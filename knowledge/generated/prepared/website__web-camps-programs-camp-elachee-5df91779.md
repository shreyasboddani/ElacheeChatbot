Camp Elachee
Where Summer is an Adventure
You’re Not Just
Looking for a Camp
the kind that smells like pine needles, sounds like laughter by the creek, and feels like freedom.
Camp Elachee isn’t about filling time. It’s about filling childhood with wonder, independence, and the joy of discovering the world outside.
At Camp Elachee, summer comes alive through forest hikes, stream exploration, outdoor science, and hands-on adventure.
Why Families Love Camp Elachee?
Here’s How to Join the Adventure
See If Your Child Qualifies By Age
Must be 5 years old on or before September 1, 2026
Register Online
Spots fill fast, secure your child’s place today.
Watch Them Thrive Outdoors
Each day is packed with discovery, growth, and joyful messes.
What’s Next at Elachee
There’s always something fresh on the calendar for first-time visitors or a longtime members. From guided hikes and nature talks to hands-on workshops and seasonal celebrations, Elachee events bring people and nature together all year long.
Hear from Parents Just Like You
First camp my twins have been to, they absolutely loved, it was a great experience, great stuff, bus driver awesome and super kind. My boys will never forget this experience, every morning they woke up excited to go and see new things. The experience on the creek it was excellent for them too. Will definitely spread the world because we loved.
