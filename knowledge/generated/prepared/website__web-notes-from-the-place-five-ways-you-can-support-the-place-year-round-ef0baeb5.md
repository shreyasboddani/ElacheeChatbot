Five Ways You Can Support The Place Year-Round
When most people think about giving back, they often think of the holidays. While the holiday season is an important time for nonprofits, the needs of our neighbors don't disappear once the decorations come down. Every day, families, seniors, and individuals in Forsyth and Dawson counties rely on The Place for food, financial assistance, education, workforce development, youth programs, and other essential services that help them move toward self-sufficiency.
Thanks to the generosity of our community, The Place has been serving local families for more than 50 years. Founded in 1975, our mission is simple yet powerful: to assist the people of Forsyth and Dawson County areas with essential needs and to be self-sustaining.
If you've been looking for meaningful ways to make an impact, here are five ways you can support The Place throughout the year.
1. Become a Monthly Donor
One of the most impactful ways to support The Place is by becoming a monthly donor through our Hearts of Impact giving program. Monthly gifts provide reliable funding that allows us to respond quickly to community needs, keep our food pantry shelves stocked, and invest in long-term programs that help individuals and families build brighter futures. Even a small recurring gift can create lasting change over time.
2. Volunteer Your Time
Volunteers are the heart of The Place. Whether you're sorting donations in our thrift store, helping distribute groceries, assisting with senior outreach, or serving at one of our special events, your time makes a direct difference in the lives of our neighbors. Whether you have a few hours each month or are looking for a regular volunteer opportunity, there's a place for everyone to serve.
3. Host a Donation or Food Drive
Looking for a way to involve your workplace, church, neighborhood, or civic organization? Hosting a food or donation drive is a fun and meaningful way to make a collective impact. The Place accepts donations of shelf-stable food, hygiene products, and other essential items that help us meet the growing needs of our community. Every donation helps ensure local families have access to the resources they need when they need them most.
4. Shop and Donate at The Place Thrift Store
Every purchase and every donated item at The Place Thrift Store helps fund programs and services that benefit our community. By shopping secondhand or donating gently used clothing, furniture, and household goods, you're giving items a second life while helping provide hope and opportunity for local families. It's a simple way to make a difference while supporting a mission you can feel good about.
5. Spread the Word
Not everyone can give financially—but everyone can help share our mission. Follow The Place on social media, share our posts, invite friends to volunteer, attend community events, or tell someone about the services we provide. Raising awareness helps connect more people to resources, recruit volunteers, and inspire others to give. Your voice can help expand our impact and ensure more neighbors know where to turn during difficult times.
Every Act of Kindness Matters
At The Place, we believe that strong communities are built when neighbors help neighbors. Whether you choose to donate, volunteer, shop, host a drive, or simply share our mission, your support helps provide food, stability, education, and hope to individuals and families throughout Forsyth and Dawson counties.
Together, we can continue to Support. Empower. Transform.—not just during the holidays, but every day of the year.
