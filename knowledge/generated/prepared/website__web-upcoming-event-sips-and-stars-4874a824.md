Sips and Stars
February 14 - May 09, 2026
Join us for Sips and Stars, a relaxed evening for adults (21+) that blends astronomy, atmosphere, and a laid-back night outdoors at Elachee.
Enjoy a glass of wine while spending time under the stars, gathered around fire pits on the back patio. Throughout the evening, guests can explore the night sky through telescopes and planetarium shows, offering a closer look at celestial sights and seasonal highlights.
Sips and Stars is designed to be casual and welcoming — the perfect way to unwind, learn something new, and enjoy the outdoors in good company. Whether you’re celebrating Valentine’s Day, Mother’s Day, or simply a night out, this program offers a unique way to connect with the night sky.
Dates:
February 14 (Valentine’s Day)
April 11
May 9 (Mother’s Day)
Ages: 21+
Date: Feb 14 - May 09, 2026

Price: $15/Member - $20/Non-Member
