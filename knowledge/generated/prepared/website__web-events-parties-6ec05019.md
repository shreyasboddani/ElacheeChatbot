Events & Parties at Elachee
Gather in Nature. Celebrate with Purpose.
At Elachee, every gathering becomes something more — an experience rooted in nature, community, and impact.
For children’s birthday parties, corporate retreats, or one of our signature events,
Elachee gives you a reason to gather and a reason to care.
Signature Events
Celebrate local wildlife with hands-on activities, animal encounters, and family-friendly fun.
A day of classic camp activities designed for grown-ups who want to explore, learn, and play outdoors.
Fresh air. Fresh thinking. Real impact.
Elachee offers a unique setting for your next team retreat, off-site, or celebration — with optional nature programming and a mission you’ll feel proud to support.
A wild celebration for curious kids.
Our nature-themed party packages are perfect for kids ages 4–10 — complete with animal encounters, guided hikes, and hands-on fun.
Why It Matters
You’re helping protect 1,440 acres of forest, support environmental education, and create moments of wonder
