## Curated public facts

Elachee's Chicopee Woods Nature Preserve has more than 12 miles of hiking trails. Trail lengths and descriptions below are from Elachee's current hiking-trails guide.

## Trail choices

- Ed Dodd Loop: 0.64-mile loop; self-guided route with some steep sections.
- Geiger Trail: 0.4-mile paved, stroller-suitable trail with storybook kiosks.
- Elachee Creek Loop: 0.42-mile loop with steep sections.
- Ridge Trail: 0.35-mile trail connecting the Ed Dodd and Bridge Loop area.
- Upland Trail: 0.65-mile trail between upland forest and Vulture Rock Creek habitat.
- Bridge Loop: 2.91-mile moderate loop with five bridged stream crossings, including a 140-foot suspension bridge.
- Lake Loop: 3-mile moderate loop near Chicopee Lake; it overlaps Bridge Loop for 0.4 mile and crosses an earthen dam.
- Chicopee Backcountry Trail: 4.26-mile moderate-to-difficult point-to-point trail with steep terrain and creek crossings. It is not a loop; plan a return trip. Trekking poles are recommended, and Elachee does not recommend hiking it in wet or poor weather conditions.

Trail access points include the gravel trailhead near the Elachee Visitor Center and Chicopee Lake. The Geiger Trail is the specific paved, stroller-suitable route; do not describe other trails as accessible without a source confirming that.

## Rules and facilities

- Elachee's current hiking guide says dogs are permitted on hiking trails on Sundays only and must follow local leash laws.
- Bikes are not permitted on the hiking trails.
- Trail maps are available online and at on-site kiosks.
- Restrooms are available at the Visitor Center during its public hours; portable toilets are at trailheads.
- There are no water fountains on the trails; bring water.
- Parking is separate from free trail access. See https://elachee.org/parking-admissions for current fees.
