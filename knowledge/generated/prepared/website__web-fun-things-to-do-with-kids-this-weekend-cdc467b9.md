Looking for Fun Things to Do with Kids This Weekend?
Looking for fun things to do with kids that are exciting, educational, and perfect for family bonding? You’re in the right place! If you’re near Gainesville, GA, Elachee Nature Science Center is a hidden gem that delivers meaningful family-friendly activities, outdoor adventure, and hands-on learning experiences — ideal for things to do with kids this weekend and beyond.
Fun Things to Do with Kids This Weekend
Elachee offers a nature-rich playground where families can explore together, discover something new, and reconnect with the outdoors. You can start your day by meeting native wildlife and exploring interactive indoor exhibits that spark curiosity in children of all ages.
From there, wander out onto winding forest trails that stretch through over 1,400 acres of protected land in the Chicopee Woods Nature Preserve — a perfect way to enjoy fresh air and make unforgettable family adventures.
Whether today’s plans were spontaneous or you’re looking for ideas for the upcoming weekend, Elachee offers delightful experiences for fun things to do with kids near me that both kids and adults will love.
Fun Things to Do with Kids Outdoors
Easy Hikes Near Me
Elachee’s network of trails includes gentle, family-friendly paths like the Dodd Loop Self-Guided Trail — perfect for little legs and first-time hikers. Along the way, interpretive signs teach kids about the forest, plants, and wildlife they see.
With over a dozen miles of trails weaving through quiet woods, wetlands, and streams, these paths are among the best easy hikes near me for a memorable outdoor morning or afternoon. Pediatric experts also encourage families to prioritize healthy outdoor activities for kids as a simple way to support physical and mental well-being while reducing screen time.
Exploration That Never Gets Old
Kids love discovery — and at Elachee, they’re free to explore nature up close. Bring a nature journal or download a trail map to turn your walk into an adventure, spotting everything from tracks to colorful leaf patterns.
Hands-On Activities Kids Love
At Elachee, learning isn’t something you sit through — it’s something you do. The center features:
Interactive exhibits where kids can learn about ecosystems and animals up close.
Live animal encounters that spark wonder and connection.
Programs like the Young Explorers Club, where kids join guided activities that blend play with learning.
Experiences like these support broader goals around environmental education in Georgia, helping children grow into thoughtful stewards of the natural world.
Programs vary by season, but every visit offers opportunities for kids to touch, observe, and explore in a supportive nature-based environment.
Educational and Fun Things to Do with Kids
Elachee has made a name for itself as a leader in environmental education for more than 45 years, combining kid-friendly attractions with meaningful learning.
Kids can engage in science-rich activities that go beyond typical museums — from learning how pollination works to discovering the animals that call North Georgia home. These immersive experiences help make complex ideas accessible and fun, perfect for curious minds of all ages.
Special events like wildlife festivals bring families together for hands-on encounters with nature’s wonders.
Affordable, Fun Things to Do with Kids
Elachee is an affordable option for quality family time, with free trail access and modest admission prices for exhibits and programs.
Bring a picnic and make a full day of it in the shaded picnic areas, or explore trails with no cost at all. Elachee’s approach to affordable outdoor fun makes it a top pick when searching for places to take kids that won’t break the bank.
Plan Your Visit for a Fun Family Day
Ready to explore Elachee with your family? Planning ahead helps you make the most of your visit:
Hours & Admission: Trails are free and open daily from dawn to sunset, while exhibits and programs run on specific schedules.
What to Bring: Pack comfy shoes, water, snacks, and curiosity!
Programs & Events: Check Elachee’s calendar for seasonal offerings like Sci-Art Saturdays and nature playgroups — engaging options for repeated family-friendly activities.
Whether you want a peaceful hike, interactive learning, or a full day of exploration with your favorite people, Elachee Nature Science Center is a wonderful choice when thinking about things to do with kids this weekend.
Experience Elachee — where every walk through the woods becomes a story your family will treasure.
