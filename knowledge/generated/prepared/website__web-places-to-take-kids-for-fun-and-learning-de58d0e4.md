Places to Take Kids for Fun and Learning
Finding the right activity for your kids shouldn’t feel like homework. When looking for places to take kids, you want something that gets them off screens, sparks genuine curiosity, and doesn’t drain your wallet, ideally all three at once.
Georgia families are lucky! The state is packed with outdoor preserves, hands-on science centers, and wildlife experiences that deliver on all counts. And if you’re in or near Gainesville, one destination stands above the rest: Elachee Nature Science Center.
Here’s a guide to some of the best places to take kids in Georgia, with a close look at what makes Elachee worth a spot on your weekend list
Fun Things to Do With Kids This Weekend in Georgia
Weekend plans don’t have to be elaborate to be memorable. Some of the best experiences for kids are the simplest: a trail to hike, a creek to explore, a bridge to cross.
At Elachee Nature Science Center, families have access to one of the largest protected forests in Northeast Georgia, the 1,440-acre Chicopee Woods Nature Preserve. With nearly 12 miles of trails winding through forests, wetlands, and streams, kids can spot wildlife, cross suspension bridges, and discover what’s living under every rock and log.
Highlights families love on a weekend visit:
Short nature hikes through wooded terrain (with wildlife sightings!)
Seasonal family events and festivals throughout the year
Indoor exhibits featuring live animals and hands-on displays
Guided nature programs for kids of all ages
For families with little ones, the paved Geiger Trail is stroller-friendly, so even toddlers can enjoy a real nature experience without the mud (unless they want the mud)!
Educational Places to Take Kids in Georgia
Families across North Georgia choose Elachee because it combines outdoor adventure with hands-on science learning.
What sets nature-based learning apart is how it sticks. Kids who learn about ecosystems while walking through one retain far more than what they’d pick up in a classroom. That’s the philosophy behind programs like Project Learning Tree, which uses forests and natural spaces as teaching tools for science and environmental literacy.
Elachee’s programs and exhibits cover:
Native plants and animals of Georgia
Forest ecosystems and how habitats work together
Conservation and environmental stewardship
Space science and exploration
The center’s curriculum is used by local schools, homeschool families, and summer camp programs, making it one of the most well-rounded educational destinations in the region.
Indoor Exhibits at Elachee: What to Expect
Elachee is more than just a few trails. Elachee’s campus features three indoor exhibits that keep kids engaged on rainy days or anytime curious minds need a new challenge.
Space Encounters
Look up! This exhibit introduces kids to the universe beyond our atmosphere, including planets, stars, galaxies, and the science of space exploration. It’s a natural starting point for kids who’ve ever stared at the night sky and asked why.
Families who want to extend the learning at home can explore NASA Space Place, a free, interactive resource designed specifically for young learners.
Woodland Wonders
Georgia’s forests are full of life, most of which goes unnoticed by the average passerby. Woodland Wonders pulls back the curtain on native wildlife, plants, insects, and soil ecosystems, showing kids how everything in a forest depends on everything else.
Kids leave this exhibit understanding:
How native wildlife and plants are connected
The role insects and decomposers play in forest health
Why biodiversity matters and what threatens it
How conservation protects the ecosystems we depend on
Want to dig deeper at home? The National Wildlife Federation offers great guides to native animals and habitats across North America.
The Cocoon
Designed especially for toddlers and their caregivers, The Cocoon offers a quiet, calming space where families can pause and recharge during their visit. Soft, interactive elements encourage gentle sensory play, giving little ones a chance to explore at their own pace while caregivers relax nearby.
In other words, if a child needs a moment to cool down or a family simply wants a peaceful break between adventures, The Cocoon provides a welcoming spot to rest, reconnect, and continue the day feeling refreshed.
Outdoor Places to Take Kids: Active Fun in Nature
The best family adventures usually involve some dirt! Outdoor exploration gives kids the kind of full-body, full-brain engagement that’s hard to replicate indoors, and research backs it up.
The CDC recommends kids get regular physical activity to support healthy growth, stronger muscles and bones, and improved focus. Trail hiking, creek exploration, and wildlife watching are all low-cost ways to meet those physical activity guidelines — while having the kind of fun kids actually talk about later.
At Elachee, suggested activities include:
Hiking nearly 12 miles of forested trails
Exploring creek beds and wetland habitats
Birdwatching and wildlife observation
Visiting the nature center exhibits mid-hike
Whether it’s your first trail walk with a toddler or your tenth visit with an elementary-aged explorer, Chicopee Woods has space for everyone.
Hands-On Activities Kids Love
Ask any child what they remember about a field trip, and they’ll tell you about the thing they touched: the snake, the butterfly, the creek rock with a crayfish underneath it.
Hands-on learning isn’t just more fun. It’s more effective. When kids interact directly with their environment, curiosity becomes the engine of education.
Popular hands-on experiences at Elachee and similar Georgia nature centers:
Animal encounters and wildlife education programs
Nature scavenger hunts on the trails
Guided hikes with naturalist educators
Creek exploration and habitat observation
Elachee also offers summer camps, homeschool programs, and family nature events throughout the year, all designed to build confidence, scientific thinking, and a genuine love of the outdoors.
Affordable Places to Take Kids in Georgia
Great family experiences don’t require a big budget. In fact, some of the most memorable outings cost almost nothing, and nature is usually at the top of that list.
What makes places like Elachee so valuable:
Low-cost admission compared to amusement parks or indoor entertainment venues
Family membership options that pay for themselves in a few visits
Free outdoor access to trails and natural areas
Seasonal programming is included with admission or membership
Elachee’s mission is to make nature education accessible to all families in the region — not just those who can afford premium experiences.
For families who plan to visit more often, Elachee also offers several membership tiers that make repeat visits even more affordable. In many cases, just two visits can cover the cost of a membership, giving families the opportunity to return throughout the year while also receiving discounts on programs and special events.
With options that include both free outdoor exploration and affordable memberships, Elachee helps ensure that nature education remains accessible for families across the region.
Plan Your Visit to Elachee Nature Science Center
If you’re searching for the best places to take kids near Gainesville, or anywhere in Northeast Georgia, Elachee is a clear standout.
From your child spotting their first salamander or finally understanding what a life cycle means, every visit offers something new to discover. Experiences like these are what make places like Elachee such a valuable resource for families in Northeast Georgia.
For families who visit often, Elachee also offers several membership options that make it easy to return throughout the year. With access to the trails, exhibits, and seasonal programs, it’s a simple way to ensure there is always something meaningful and active to do with your kids.
