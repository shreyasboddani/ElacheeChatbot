Host A Holiday House Drive
Make a difference for local families this holiday season by organizing a Holiday House drive with your school, church, workplace, neighborhood, team, or community group. Collect new, unwrapped gifts for children (ages 0-18), $25 gifts cards, or holiday essentials like wrapping paper, gift bags, bows, and tape.
Whether you're planning a service project, friendly competition, or community-wide collection, a Holiday House drive is a simple way to bring people together and support local parents and guardians as they create aa holiday experience for their children. Complete the form below to get started.
