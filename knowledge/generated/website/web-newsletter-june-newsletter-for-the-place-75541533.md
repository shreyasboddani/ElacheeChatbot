June 2026 Newsletter for The Place
Summer Snack Packs
We are so thankful for our community stepping up to help us meet the need of our students over summer break!
When school is out, many children lose access to the meals they rely on throughout the week.Thanks to the generosity of individuals, businesses, churches, and community groups, we have been able to provide snack packs to help fill that gap and ensure local children have food during the summer months.So far this summer, we have given away over 1,000 snack packs at the Mobile Food Pantries at local schools.
From hosting donation and snack pack building events to filling collection bins and making financial gifts, your support is making a real difference for families in Forsyth and Dawson counties.While we are incredibly grateful for the support we've received so far, the need continues throughout the summer.We are still looking for community partners, businesses, churches, and civic groups willing to host drives, donate snacks, or make financial contributions to help us keep these shelves stocked.
Together, we can ensure local children have access to food while school is out and families don't have to focus on where the next meal will come from.
The Place PodcastWant to learn more about our Food Pantry in Dawson?In this episode of The Place Podcast, Nicole visits the Dawson Food Pantry and talks with Andy Ferrell, Food Pantry Volunteer, to talk about how The Place is stepping up during the summer months to help meet the increased need.
Drawing from his firsthand experience serving in the Dawson Food Pantry and at Mobile Pantries, Andy shares what he has learned about the realities of food insecurity in Dawson County and the impact access to food can have on families facing difficult circumstances.
The conversation also explores the unique challenges of summer, when children lose access to school meals and many families struggle to stretch already tight grocery budgets. Andy discusses how The Place responds through our Food Pantry, Mobile Pantry program, and Summer Feeding efforts, and why community support is more important than ever.
Whether you're interested in volunteering, donating, or simply learning more about the need in our area, this episode offers an inspiring look at how neighbors helping neighbors can make a lasting difference.
