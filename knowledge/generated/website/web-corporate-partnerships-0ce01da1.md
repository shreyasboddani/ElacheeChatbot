Corporate Partnerships with Elachee
Wellness with Purpose
In today’s world, stress is constant and screen time is endless. Most workplace wellness perks feel like just another app or login. But what if your team’s best medicine is a walk in the woods? At Elachee, we believe the natural world has a way of putting things back into balance and we want to share that with you.
Let Nature Do What It Does Best
Elachee Nature Science Center isn’t just a place to visit, it’s 1,440 acres of quiet pine forests, walking trails, fresh air, and possibility, and it’s right here in your community.
When your business partners with Elachee, you’re giving employees more than a benefit. You’re offering a reset button, a way to slow down, breathe deep, and reconnect.
How It Works
Pick your Partnership Plan
Choose between a flexible quarterly or full-year membership.
Empower your Team
Each participating employee gets their own Elachee membership, parking pass, and access to curated wellness challenges that fit their lifestyle.
Host a day in the woods
We’ll reserve a private meeting space for your team. It’s perfect for a retreat, brainstorm, or simply getting out of the office and into something real.

Wellness That Does More
By choosing Elachee, you’re investing in your people and the planet.
Your support fuels:
Environmental education for 35,000+ Georgia students
Wildlife conservation in one of the state’s most biodiverse regions
Trail maintenance and outdoor access for your whole community
This isn’t a transaction. It’s a relationship with nature, with your community, and with what matters.
