2026 YEAR-END GIVING
Invest in Wonder
Your 2026 year-end gift carries Elachee’s work into 2027, supporting the people, places, and experiences that connect our community with nature and science.
Four Ways You Can Invest in Wonder
The Access Fund helps ensure that cost is not a barrier to experiencing Elachee.
The Wonder Fund supports growth and enhancement of Elachee’s exhibits and learning spaces.
The Annual Fund supports the full mission of Elachee (funding for animals, trails, education programs & daily operations).
The Stewardship Fund supports the care and protection of the 1,440-acre Chicopee Woods Preserve.
CHOOSE WHERE YOUR GIFT GOES
Direct your gift to the fund that means the most to you: Annual, Access, Wonder, or Stewardship. Your selection tells us where you would most like to make an impact. A gift of any size helps carry this work into 2027.
Why Your Gift Matters
Your generosity brings Elachee’s mission to life, connecting students with hands-on nature and science experiences, caring for our animal ambassadors, and protecting the trails and natural spaces our community loves.
