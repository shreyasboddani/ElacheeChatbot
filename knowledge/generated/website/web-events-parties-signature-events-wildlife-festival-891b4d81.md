Elachee Wildlife Festival
Wild Encounters. Real Connection. Unforgettable Fun.
Join us for a one-of-a-kind day where families, nature lovers, and curious minds gather to meet wildlife, explore science, and celebrate the natural world we all share.
Saturday, September 12th, 2026 10am-2pm
More Than a Festival —
You’re not just looking for something to do. You want a reason to get outside, to connect, to see your kids light up as they meet a hawk, a turtle, or a beekeeper for the first time.
Wildlife Festival is Elachee’s biggest event of the year — and one of the best ways to experience everything we’re about in a single, unforgettable day.
Why It Matters
When you attend Elachee’s Wildlife Festival, you’re not just joining an event — you’re stepping into an experience. One that connects people to nature, sparks curiosity, and strengthens a shared commitment to protect what matters. Elachee is more than trails and ticket discounts. It’s a community effort — one that cares for 1,440 acres of protected forest, safeguards native wildlife, and ensures the next generation grows up with their feet on the trail and wonder in their eyes.
Your ticket supports hands-on science programs, live animal exhibits, and the kinds of moments that ignite discovery and shape young minds. It helps keep nature wild and welcoming — not just for today, but for the future.
Find Your Way
Back to the Woods
Because nature isn’t just for kids. It’s for anyone who needs a reset, a breath of fresh air, and a day that actually feels like summer.
What’s Happening at Elachee
Our recurring experiences invite you to return again and again, with the individual sessions that explore science, nature, creativity, and discovery.
