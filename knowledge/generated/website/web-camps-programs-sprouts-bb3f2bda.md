Sprouts Nature Program
Nature Play for Curious Toddlers
Nature Is
The Classroom
Sprouts is your reminder to unplug and reconnect. Sprouts, Elachee’s monthly nature play program for toddlers (ages 18 months to 6 years) and their caregivers, blends movement, music, storytelling, and sensory exploration in the great outdoors. Each playful, hands-on session helps build your child’s language, motor skills, confidence, and love for the natural world, all in a safe, welcoming environment.
Sprouts is more than a one-time outing, it’s a monthly memory-maker. Caregivers and kids return again and again to reconnect, learn, and grow side by side. Whether it’s spotting a squirrel or singing your child’s new favorite song, every visit offers something new to see, touch, and celebrate.
Here’s How It Works
Check Our Open House

Come Explore
Dress for the weather, bring your curiosity, and get ready to sing, play, and discover nature together.
