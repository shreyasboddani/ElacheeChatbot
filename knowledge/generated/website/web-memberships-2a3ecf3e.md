Become a Member at Elachee
An Elachee membership is more than a pass, it’s your invitation to connect with nature, support conservation, and make unforgettable memories. From exploring the trails of Chicopee Woods, engaging with live science exhibits, or just finding space to slow down with your family, your membership helps keep these experiences thriving for everyone.
What You Get as a Member
Free admission to Elachee Nature Science Center and Chicopee Woods Area Park.
Member are provided annual parking passes at all levels of membership.
Select member levels receive discounts and early registration access on all Elachee programs and camps.
Membership Levels
Explorer
Annual parking pass
Unlimited self-guided general admission
Two (2) guest passes
Reciprocal benefits at more than 150 nature centers nationwide
Discovery
All Explorer benefits
10% off select programs
Early registration for Camp Elachee
10% camp discount Four (4) guest passes
Discovery Plus
All Discovery benefits
20% off select programs
20% camp discount
Six (6) guest passes
Access to one (1) Premium Members-only event or experience
A free Elachee- branded item
Memberships provide vital support for:
Environmental education for over 35,000 students annually
Stewardship of the 1,440-acre Chicopee Woods Nature Preserve
Community programs, field trips, and nature-based learning
Looking for a meaningful gift? Elachee memberships make lasting memories—perfect for birthdays, holidays, or “just because.”
Join the Elachee Community
A Elachee Nature Science Center membership is a way to stay connected, support environmental education, and enjoy meaningful experiences throughout the year.
