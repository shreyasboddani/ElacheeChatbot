Heartsgiving
Heartsgiving is The Place’s newest holiday tradition, bringing Thanksgiving meals, warm coats, and holiday cheer to more than 450 local families.
The week before Thanksgiving, families who regularly visit The Place’s Food Pantry will receive everything they need to help make their holiday special, including:
