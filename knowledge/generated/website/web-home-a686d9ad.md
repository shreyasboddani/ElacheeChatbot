Educate. Explore. Escape.
Experience Elachee
Step into a world where curiosity comes naturally, relaxation runs wild, and trails lead to wonder.
If you’re a parent planning your child’s next outdoor adventure, an educator seeking hands-on teaching, or simply ready to unplug, Elachee Nature Science Center in Gainesville, GA is your place to discover, learn, and play.
Parents & Families
Explore nature together with camps, weekend programs, and seasonal events.
Educators
Standards-aligned field trips and outreach programs that ignite curiosity and make science unforgettable.
Visitors & Nature Lovers
Stroll the trails, visit our live animals, or just take a breath, there’s always something to discover.
Miles of Trails. Endless Wonder.
Step into the heart of the 1,440-acre Chicopee Woods Nature Preserve and experience over 12 miles of scenic trails. Whether you’re chasing a peaceful moment or leading a pack of curious kids, Elachee’s winding paths take you through quiet forests, vibrant wetlands, and along whispering streams. From gentle loops made for little legs to backcountry trails that test your stride, every route offers a chance to slow down, tune in, and rediscover the wild right outside your door.
Learning That’s Full of Life
This isn’t learning from a desk, it’s nature-powered, curiosity-driven discovery made for all ages.
Our indoor and outdoor exhibits invite curiosity at every turn. Come eye-to-eye with native animals, peer into the world of pollinators, trace the trees of Georgia, and explore nature through play. No matter your age or stage of life, there is something for you to enjoy at Elachee Nature Science Center.
A nurturing space where young minds grow and transform through play, calm, and nature-inspired wonder.
An immersive indoor classroom that invites young learners to discover Georgia’s wild wonders.
Blast off into the cosmos with hands-on exhibits that spark curiosity about planets, stars, and our place in the universe.
Camps & Programs
Experiences & Adventures for Every Age
At Elachee, we believe children grow best when they grow outside. Our camps and programs are designed to meet every age and stage — nurturing wonder in toddlers, sparking discovery in homeschoolers, and building lifelong memories for summer campers.
From seasonal playgroups to science-rich activities, every experience invites your child to think boldly, explore freely, and fall in love with the natural world.
Monthly nature play sessions for toddlers (18 months – 4 years).
A full-time, Cognia-accredited school for preschool through early elementary. Inquiry-led, nature-based learning five days a week.
Weeklong summer day camps for Kindergarten through middle school. Outdoor fun, science discovery, and classic summer memories.
Monthly science-based field classes for K–12 homeschool families.Aligned with Georgia standards and rooted in hands-on outdoor learning.
Elachee is More Than a Place, It’s a Purpose
The Elachee Mission
Elachee Nature Science Center promotes environmental understanding through education and conservation.
Your Membership Makes a Difference
Becoming a member of Elachee is more than access to trails, discounts, or early event invites. It’s a way to do your part — to care for a 1,440-acre forest, protect wildlife habitats, and ensure the next generation grows up with their feet on the trail and joy in their eyes.
Your membership supports hands-on science programs, live animal exhibits, and moments of awe that shape young minds. It helps keep nature wild and welcoming for everyone.

Your support fuels Elachee’s mission year-round
Elachee Nature Science Center is a trusted leader in environmental education and conservation, rooted in the 1,440-acre Chicopee Woods Nature Preserve.
For more than 40 years, Elachee has connected people of all ages to the natural world through immersive science programs, outdoor experiences, and a deep commitment to protecting Georgia’s wild places.
We believe in growing curiosity and keeping nature at the heart of every community’s story.

Memorable
Make it a birthday they’ll never forget — wild, wonder-filled, and one-of-a-kind. Elachee’s themed party packages include hands-on nature activities, and unforgettable encounters with live animal ambassadors. It’s the perfect blend of celebration, curiosity, and connection to the natural world.
Refreshing
Take your team beyond the boardroom—into a setting that inspires. Surrounded by the natural beauty of Chicopee Woods, Elachee offers flexible meeting spaces, fresh-air thinking, and optional guided programs that foster connection, creativity, and clarity. It’s a retreat that feels like a reset.
