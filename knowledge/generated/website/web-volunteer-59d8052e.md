Volunteer with Elachee
At Elachee Nature Science Center, volunteers are essential partners in our mission. Whether you’re maintaining trails, supporting science
programs, assisting with events, or caring for our live animals, your time and talent help Elachee thrive.
Looking for a meaningful way to give back?
Join a dedicated community of nature lovers, educators, and conservation advocates. As a volunteer, you’ll make a tangible difference while gaining experience, spending time outdoors, and connecting with like-minded individuals.
Volunteer with Elachee
Fill out the form below to get involved as an individual volunteer and support Elachee’s mission through hands-on service and meaningful work.
Volunteer Groups
Elachee partners with schools, churches, civic clubs, and other organizations to create impactful group volunteer opportunities. Whether you’re clearing trails, assisting with educational programs, or helping with habitat restoration, your group can make a tangible difference in just a few hours.
Interested in scheduling a group volunteer experience?
Please complete the form below, and a member of our team will follow up to coordinate details and match your group with a project that fits.
Why It Matters
Because your time supports conservation, education, and community. Volunteers are at the heart of what we do — helping Elachee serve more than 35,000 students and visitors each year while preserving one of Georgia’s largest urban forests.
This is your chance to make a real and lasting impact.
