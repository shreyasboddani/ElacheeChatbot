Elachee for Grown-Ups
Let Nature Nurture Your Spirit
At Elachee, we believe nature isn’t just for kids and classrooms, it’s for anyone who needs space to breathe, time to reflect, and a reason to reconnect.
You’re Not
Looking For a Lecture —
You’re Looking for Peace
You don’t need a packed itinerary. You need permission to pause. Elachee’s grown-up programs are built around simplicity, curiosity, and presence, giving you time to notice the birdsong, feel the breeze, and remember what it’s like to be fully here. Whether you come with questions, a good book, or just to be present with nature, there’s space for you.
Why It Matters
Elachee’s adult programs are more than a way to relax, they’re an invitation to reconnect with the natural world and your place in it.
Every moment you spend here supports a greater mission, helping protect 1,440 acres of forest and inspire the next generation of environmental stewards.
