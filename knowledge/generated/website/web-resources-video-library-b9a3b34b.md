Nature in Motion: Stories from Elachee
Our video gallery brings the Elachee mission to life.
Watch hands-on science, forest adventures, and real
connections unfold — because nature isn’t just meant to be
seen. It’s meant to be felt.
Elachee Nature Science Center offers engaging adult learning opportunities that connect participants with nature, science, and environmental stewardship through hands-on programs, expert-led discussions, outdoor experiences, and lifelong learning activities designed to inspire curiosity, understanding, and appreciation of the natural world.
Planning your visit to Elachee? Watch this video to help guide you on your journey! Getting here can be a little confusing the first time, so follow along for helpful landmarks and directions to make your arrival easy and stress-free.
Each year, more than 7,000 school children in North Georgia engage in interactive astronomy experiences through Elachee Nature Science Center’s accredited field trip and outreach programs that utilize the Starlab portable planetarium.
Climb aboard the Lake Lanier Aquatic Learning Center (LLALC) Floating Classroom, Chota Princess II. Elachee instructors guide students in a hands-on exploration of the history, ecology, and challenges facing Lake Sidney Lanier. On shore, Elachee instructors engage students in related watershed activities.
Camp Elachee invites children to discover the wonders of nature through outdoor adventures, hands-on science, wildlife exploration, and creative play. Campers build confidence, make new friends, and develop a lifelong appreciation for the natural world in a fun, supportive environment.
Elachee is a place of wonder, but wonder doesn’t fund itself. We rely on your support to keep our trails open, programs thriving, and curiosity alive. Watch to learn how your donation makes a lasting impact on nature, education, and our community.
Elachee is more than just a walk in the woods, it’s a place to reconnect with nature, with others, and with ourselves. In a tech-filled world, Elachee offers space to learn, explore, and breathe. Hear from community voices on why this special place matters now more than ever.
We’ve got a new look! Elachee Nature Science Center is unveiling a fresh logo, website, and signage that will reflect our mission of curiosity, care, and community. Same values, new visuals. Come see what’s next!
Join us October 23–26, 2025, for Back to Nature Weekend at Elachee! Celebrate our newly renovated campus with events for everyone—adult-only nights, stargazing, family fun, and educator experiences. Come see what’s new and rediscover what makes Elachee feel like home.
