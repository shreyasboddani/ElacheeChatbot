Visit Elachee Today
Ignite Wonder
Meet native animals, explore hands-on science, and more. Elachee’s exhibits spark discovery for kids, families, and curious minds of all ages.
Embrace Wonder
Twelve miles of winding trails and room to roam. Hike the Chicopee Woods and feel the pace of life shift while you chase an adventure or are just catching your breath.

What to Bring
Make a day of it! Enjoy your meal at one of our shaded picnic areas, on the Visitor Center patio, or at the Nature Nook reading platform. (And remember, pack it in, pack it out.)
We’re a forest, after all — best to come prepared.
You’ll be walking trails, climbing logs, and exploring outdoor play spaces. Dress for movement and maybe a little mud.
The most important thing to pack. From spotting a turtle to learning how bees work, there’s always something new to discover.
Hats, sunscreen, and rain jackets help keep your day comfortable. No matter what we’ll be open rain or shine.
There are no water fountains on the trails, so bring a refillable bottle and a few energizing snacks.
Plan Your Day at Elachee
Because curious minds deserve big adventures.
Pick Your Day
Elachee is open year-round (excluding major holidays), making it ideal for both spontaneous outings and planned adventures. Weekdays offer a quiet, relaxed atmosphere, perfect for stroller strolls and unhurried play. On Saturdays, the experience comes alive with live animal encounters, programs in our discovery dome, and hands-on activities for the whole family.
Start at the Visitor Center
Don’t forget to check out a Discovery Backpack to turn your walk into a nature scavenger hunt packed with tools and activity prompts that keep little explorers engaged.
Let Curiosity Lead the Way
Peek at turtles, wander the trails, build a stick fort, or play giant games at the Creation Station. There’s no schedule, just freedom to explore.
Break for Lunch (and a Breather)
Bring a packed lunch and relax at our shaded picnic areas, Visitor Center patio, or quiet Nature Nook reading platform. Don’t forget to pack wipes and a few extra snacks!
Head Home with Happy Kids
Expect muddy shoes and stories that start with “Did you see that?!”

Exciting Events. One-of-a-Kind
Elachee Experiences
Wildlife Festival
Get up close with reptiles, raptors, and more. Perfect for animal lovers of all ages, this festival offers interactive experiences and a petting zoo.
Camp Elachee for Adults
Embrace your inner child with activities like archery, live music, and guided night hikes. It’s camp — Elachee style.
Trillium Trek
Shake off winter on the trails of Chicopee Woods with 5K, 10K, and half-marathon options of a race that winds through scenic overlooks, creek crossings, and the season’s first trillium blooms.
What’s Happening at Elachee
Our recurring experiences invite you to return again and again, with the individual sessions that explore science, nature, creativity, and discovery.
