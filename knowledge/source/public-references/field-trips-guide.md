Elachee school field trips serve Pre-K through grade 12 and are designed around Georgia Standards of Excellence. On-site programs offer hands-on outdoor learning about animals, astronomy, geology, Indigenous People of Georgia, plants, and water; some programs include live animal encounters.

## Formats and seasonality

- On-site field trips take place at Elachee in Chicopee Woods.
- Elachee in Your Classroom/outreach brings selected programs to a school. Elachee says its service area is within 60 miles or one hour of its Gainesville campus; additional fees may apply.
- The Floating Classroom offers Lake Lanier aquatic-study experiences from River Forks Park.
- Some astronomy programs are offered November through February. Some water programs are offered March through November. Check each program's current listing for exact dates and availability.

To plan a school trip, choose a topic and grade-level program, review the current Field Trip Options, then use Elachee's Plan Your Field Trip request form to ask about dates. Do not guess pricing, availability, or booking confirmation; those details must come from Elachee's current registration or staff.

Official overview and request form: https://elachee.org/field-trips
