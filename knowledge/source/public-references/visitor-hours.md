Elachee's Visitor Center and the Chicopee Woods Nature Preserve trails have different schedules.

## Visitor Center and exhibits

- Wednesday through Friday: 12:00 p.m. to 3:00 p.m.
- Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
- Sunday, Monday, and Tuesday: closed.

## Hiking trails

The Chicopee Woods Nature Preserve trails are open daily from 7:00 a.m. until sunset. Trail access is separate from Visitor Center and exhibit access: trails can be open when the Visitor Center is closed.

Elachee notes that Visitor Center opening days or hours may differ during school holidays. Check the current Elachee events calendar for special opening dates before planning a date-specific visit.

Official schedule: https://elachee.org/hours
